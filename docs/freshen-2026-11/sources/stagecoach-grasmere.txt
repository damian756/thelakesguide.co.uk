Grasmere Buses - Explore the area during your visit
Grasmere, a popular village in the Lake District and home to William Wordsworth. Nestled amongst the history and beauty of the area, there’s lots of independent shops and eateries to keep all ages entertained.
A must see is Wordsworth Grasmere, discover Dove Cottage and William and Dorothy’s story, and a must taste is the world-famous Sarah Nelson’s Grasmere Gingerbread.
Want to explore beyond Grasmere? Head north on the 555 to discover the popular market town of Keswick, have your camera at the ready as you travel along one of England's most scenic bus routes. Alternatively, travel south on the 555 or 599 towards Ambleside, Windermere or Bowness, where there won't be a shortage of shops to browse or eateries to enjoy!
