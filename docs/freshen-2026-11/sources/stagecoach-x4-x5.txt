Cumbria and North Lancashire
The X4/X5 Service
X4/X5 bus between Penrith and Workington
Connecting the East and West Lakes
Travelling to the Lakes by train? The X4/X5 Stagecoach bus route is just the ticket to start your holiday in the North Lakes.
With frequent connecting services from mainline Penrith Station operating 7 days a week throughout the year (Including Bank Holidays, except Christmas Day & New Year's Day).
This popular bus route also connects with many tourist attractions in the region, including Rheged, Keswick, The Lakes Distillery, Bassenthwaite Village, and Cockermouth.
For those that love an itinerary, click here  for your very own pre planned day out on the X4/X5! Visiting all the museum hotspots along the scenic route, it's a perfect day out for those who love to devour the delights of Cumbrian life, history and whisky too!

Workington to...

Cockermouth
19 mins by bus
A vibrant market town, steeped in history, with many antique shops and galleries. Positioned on the River Cocker, it is the birthplace of Wordsworth and Fletcher Christian.
Keswick
55 mins by bus
Head to Keswick for stunning lake views and awe-inspiring vistas. Grab an explorer and hop on and off with ease all day!
Penrith
every 30 mins
Hop off at the Rheged Centre , near Penrith, for activities and events to suit the whole family  including indoor softplay, artisan cafes and a huge 3D cinema screen. Head into Penrith itself for specialist shops, high street  favourites and arcades. You’ll find a lively farmers’ market each month, with fresh local  produce and tasty delicacies.
Penrith to...
Scroll table horizontally to view table content
Keswick
45 mins by bus
In the town you’ll discover unique  local shops, galleries and tempting cafes and restaurants.  For panoramic views, take a stroll along Friar’s Crag before catching a performance at the Theatre by the Lake .
Cockermouth
every 30 mins
Hop off at The Lake District  Wildlife Park and take a walk on the wild side with anything from alpacas to zebras.  Or if you fancy a tipple, pop round the corner to The Lakes Distillery and sample some of their award  winning products (over 18s only).
Workington
every 30 mins
For a fascinating day of industrial history, head to the Western Lakes and explore the museums. Visit the Helena Thompson Museum , a gem of a museum housing the social and industrial history of the area.
