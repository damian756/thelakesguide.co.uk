Combine stunning scenery with this cruise on the north of Windermere
Experience the beauty of Windermere’s northern shoreline on this scenic round-trip cruise from Bowness. Choose to enjoy the full 75 minute non-landing journey, or hop on and off at key destinations along the way.
More info
This beautiful round trip from Bowness can be taken as a 75 minute non-landing cruise.
Alternatively, you may board at either Ambleside or Brockhole and return on a later boat from Bowness. Your ticket allows you to get off at each stop and catch a later boat back.
Each cruise includes a fascinating commentary on the history, attractions and points of interest surrounding Windermere.
Our smaller boats called modern launches (marked red on the timetable below) stop at Brockhole on the eastern side of the lake on their way up to Ambleside.
For a great day out visit Brockhole that includes attractions for the whole family. Boat passengers enjoy free entry to the grounds.

Departure Points
Start this cruise from any of these locations
Ambleside
Located at the northern tip of Windermere, Ambleside (Waterhead) Pier offers some of the most stunning views of the Lake District fells.
The pier's postcode is LA22 0EY . It's an easy, flat walk just a half-mile from Ambleside village center, with regular buses also available.
While the pier has a small car park, there are over 200 public pay-and-display spaces within a five-minute walk. You'll also find easy access to local bus connections.

Brockhole
Brockhole is more than just a pier; it's the perfect destination for family fun - lakeshore walks, adventure play areas and Zip World .
The pier's closest postcode is LA23 1LJ but is only accessible by gravel footpath at the bottom of Brockhole's gardens.
The on-site car park can get busy, but it has great bus connections with a bus top outside on the busy A591 - about a 10-minute walk from our pier on the lakeshore.

Bowness
As our busiest pier, Bowness offers the widest variety of cruises on the lake.
Located at postcode LA23 3HQ , it's a 2-minute walk from Bowness village and the closest pier to Windermere Village (10 minutes by bus/car or a 25-minute walk).
While we don't have our own parking, there are over 400 pay-and-display spaces within walking distance. You'll also find easy access to bus connections and national rail services via Windermere Railway Station.
