Just a short drive from Penrith, our welcoming inn combines the charm of a traditional country pub with the comforts of a modern Ullswater hotel Lake District visitors return to. Whether you’re planning a walking holiday, short break or longer stay, everything you need is right on your doorstep.
As a hotel in Glenridding with a cosy, dog-friendly pub at its heart, we serve delicious, home-cooked food and drinks all day—perfect for refuelling after exploring. From hikes up Helvellyn to lakeside walks along the Ullswater Way or steamer cruises, adventure comes as standard.
Pull up a seat, soak in sweeping lake views, and unwind in a relaxed setting that makes The Ullswater Inn a standout choice for Glenridding accommodation.

Rooms at The Ullswater Inn
Check in from 3pm
Wake up in comfort at our Ullswater accommodation, where a choice of stylish double, twin and family rooms – many with stunning lake and fell views – offers the perfect base for your Lake District escape.
Each of our 56 en-suite bedrooms at The Ullswater Inn is designed with relaxation in mind, combining modern amenities with a cosy, welcoming feel. Whether you’re planning a romantic break, a family getaway or a walking holiday near Penrith, you’ll find everything you need for a restful night’s sleep after a day exploring.
Our dog friendly hotel in Ullswater also means your four-legged companion can join the adventure. With dedicated dog-friendly rooms available, you’ll never have to leave them behind. Simply add this option during the booking process, where up to two dogs can stay with you for just £15 per night.
Pair a great night’s sleep with hearty food and drinks served daily in our Glenridding pub, and you’ve got everything you need for a memorable stay in the Lake District.

The Ullswater Inn FAQs
Make your trip to The Ullswater Inn, smooth and stress-free by exploring our FAQs. You’ll find helpful information about location, parking options, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities.
Do you have on site parking available?
There is limited, free on site car parking available at The Ullswater Inn based on a first come, first served basis. There is a 24 hour, chargeable car park across the road from our inn.
Close
What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at The Ullswater Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.
Close
Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
The Ullswater Inn is DDA compliant but not easily accessible. There is disabled access to some of the lower ground floor bedrooms and to the lower bar area. Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm. There is a small lift to the upper bar/main restaurant but this cannot fit a wheelchair. For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Are dogs allowed at The Ullswater Inn?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Ullswater Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms across our inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so our staff members are aware before your arrival. Your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per pet, per night.
Simply add this on during the online booking process to ensure you get a dog friendly room, or for more information on our dog-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.
Close
What facilities are provided in the rooms?
Our 56 en-suite bedrooms provide everything you need for a restful stay, including options for doubles, twins, and family rooms. All rooms at The Ullswater Inn provide complimentary Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV, and tea & coffee making facilities.  Many rooms feature stunning views of Ullswater and the surrounding fells. For guests with four-legged friends, our dog-friendly rooms in Glenridding ensure pets are welcome too (£15 per dog, per night).
Please note, we do not provide travel cots. If you plan on bringing your own, we recommend speaking with our reservations team on 0191 580 3610 to ensure the room you’re booking will comfortably accommodate one.
Dining is available all day in our cosy pub, where we serve hearty pub fayre, locally sourced dishes, and refreshing drinks, including local ales and wines. Additional facilities include free WiFi, on-site parking (limited, available on a first come first served basis), and flexible spaces for families, couples, or walkers returning from the fells.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Ullswater Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.   We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Do you take table bookings?
Table bookings can be made online at The Ullswater Inn by clicking here .  Walk-ins are always welcome so please pop in, we’d love to see you!
Table Booking Terms & Conditions:
Advance bookings: Tables can be reserved online from until 3 hours before the selected booking time.
Walk-ins: Walk-ins are always welcome and subject to availability.
Deposits: In some cases, a deposit may be required (if table is over 8 people) . This is managed directly by the individual inn, and a member of the team will be in touch if a deposit is needed.
Arrival time: Tables will be held for 15 minutes after the booked time. After this, the table may be released.
Larger groups: Bookings for 9 or more guests will be submitted as an enquiry. The relevant inn will review your enquiry and accept or reject based on availability.
Cancellations: Please let us know if you need to cancel your booking. Advance notice helps us plan and offer the table to other guests.

What things to do in Glenridding?
Glenridding, at the southern tip of Ullswater, is the perfect base for exploring the Lake District, offering a mix of outdoor adventures, scenic walks, and relaxing activities. Staying at The Ullswater Inn, a top hotel in Glenridding, puts you right in the heart of it all.
For walkers and hikers, the nearby Ullswater Way offers a stunning circular route around the lake, while more challenging hikes, like Helvellyn, reward you with breathtaking panoramic views. If you prefer a gentler pace, enjoy a peaceful Ullswater steamer cruise or take a lakeside stroll to soak in the scenery.
Families and dog owners will love the accessible paths and local attractions, including cycling trails, boat hire, and nearby dog-friendly pubs in Ullswater. After a day of adventure, return to our cosy inn for hearty pub fare, local ales, and sweeping views of the fells.
Whether you’re searching for things to do in Ullswater or planning a short break near Penrith, Glenridding has something for everyone – making The Ullswater Inn the perfect spot to rest and recharge.
