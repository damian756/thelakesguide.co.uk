Explore the Lake District by Bus
Welcome to the Lake District, a landscape of breath-taking beauty, from soaring fells to serene, shimmering lakes. Imagine exploring it all without the stress of navigating narrow country roads or the endless search for a parking space. With Stagecoach, you can sit back, relax, and soak in the panoramic views from the top deck of a bus, turning your journey into an unforgettable part of the adventure.
Whether you're planning a family day out, a challenging hike up a famous peak, or a peaceful tour of charming villages, our extensive network is your ticket to freedom. Let us be your driver as you discover the heart of this UNESCO World Heritage Site. So join us for the cheaper, greener, and stress free way to tour the Lake District.

Lake District Bus & Boat Tickets 🛥️
We have combined  Lake District Bus & Boat tickets  which are available for Coniston, Derwentwater, Ullswater and Windermere.
Tickets are for a boat ride and a day's unlimited bus travel in the North West Explorer Zone.

Tickets🎫
No booking required. Simply purchase your tickets on our app, or on the bus by card or cash. Single journeys are capped at £3* as part of the DfT fare cap.
For £12.50 a day, our North West Explorer ticket gives you the freedom to explore more!  Plus, English concessionary pass holders can travel for FREE*!
