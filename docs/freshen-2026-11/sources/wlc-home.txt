Explore Windermere
Experience Windermere, England's largest lake, from the water.
Enjoy a relaxing journey aboard our scenic sightseeing cruises – ranging from quick jaunts to all-day explorations with a Freedom of the Lake ticket.
Or embrace the freedom of being your own captain with our popular motor boat and row boat hire .
Windermere Lake Cruises offers unforgettable ways to explore the iconic Lake District landscape.

Electric Motor Boat Hire
Explore Windermere with ease and enjoy a fun, safe day out for the whole family in our modern electric motor boats . Simple to operate and available from Bowness Beach, Bowness Dock, Waterhead, and Lakeside, they're perfect for discovering the lake's beauty.
Easy-to-drive and no experience necessary
Up to six people on board
Roll-back hoods for all-weather exploring
Hire from £46

Rowing Boats
Explore Windermere's beauty in a uniquely romantic way aboard a traditional clinker-design rowing boat . These classic vessels, available from Bowness Beach or Waterhead, have graced the lake's waters since the 1800s and carefully maintained ever since.
Enjoy a slower pace to take in the stunning views
Full training given and buoyancy aids provided
Up to two people on board (four normally, but two due to low water levels)
Hire from £28
