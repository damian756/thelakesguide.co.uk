Discover the enchanting world of Beatrix Potter in our unique Attraction – the only one of its kind in the world. Experience walking through the pages of the famous stories as Peter Rabbit and his friends are brought to life in 3D in a Lake District landscape. With a free activity trail for children, a themed Peter Rabbit ™ café, two world-famous gift shops and a full line up of events, everyone can enjoy a bit of Beatrix Potter magic, whatever the weather!

The World of Beatrix Potter ™ is a vibrant family Attraction bringing to life Beatrix Potter’s enchanting stories in a magical recreation of the beautiful Lake District countryside.
Learn more
Browse for souvenirs in one of our famous Beatrix Potter gift shops. Find us on the main street Crag Brow, or at the end of the Attraction and take a little bit of Potter magic home with you! Entry to the shops is free.
Learn more
The Peter Rabbit ™ Café offers a perfectly themed and unique dining experience with a good dose of mischief & a treasure trove of adorable treats on the menu from your favourite characters. Open daily 10.30am – 4.00pm for brunch, lunch & afternoon tea.
Learn more
The Peter Rabbit Garden
The garden tells the story of Beatrix Potter the naturalist and is designed to capture the very essence of Beatrix Potter’s world, from the cos lettuces that Benjamin Bunny nibbled on to the gooseberry bush where Peter got caught in a net as he tried to escape Mr. McGregor.
Learn more
Just outside the front of the Attraction is our boutique nursery Shop which stocks a huge range of ethical, sustainable and environmentally friendly clothing, gifts and accessories.
Learn more
The Theatre produces a lively programme of professional drama, classical music, folk, comedy, lectures and film. This intimate little theatre shares a building in Bowness with the World of Beatrix Potter Attraction.
