3 questions to ask yourself before you set off
If you score 3/3 on these questions, oﬀ you go, have a fantastic day! If not, read on to find the answers you need to be kitted up and in the know to be safe!
Am I confident I have the knowledge & skills  for the day?
Adventure allows us to step outside our immediate comfort zone and is a great way to repeatedly rediscover a zest for life. Being AdventureSmart simply means that you are thinking about your own experience and skills. Choosing an adventure that you know that is within your skillset is part of the fun – and if you want to do something that pushes beyond this, there are many ways to find a guide or instructor to help you. Read more…
Do I know what the  weather  will be like?
As we are all used to in the UK, the weather has the potential to make or spoil your day. This doesn’t have to mean that a spot of drizzle or even a howling gale has to stop us in our tracks. Like a good Scout, being prepared and adapting your plans is the key to being in control of your day. Check the weather forecast – the Met Office is a good place to start. PS Remember that most unusual occurrence of a cloudless sky can also cause problems (take care to avoid heat stroke!). Read more…
Do I have the right  gear?
If that has prompted you to ask ‘what is the right gear?’ then you need help! Kit doesn’t need to be expensive but does need to keep you warm and dry and, in the case of boots, needs to ﬁt well; there is nothing like a blister to ruin a good day’s walking! If your adventure involves heading out on the water then a well ﬁtted and well-maintained buoyancy aid is essential. This week is a scorcher 🌞, so whatever your plans, #BeAdventureSmart and ask yourself 3 questions before you set off…
1) Do I have the knowledge and skills to stay safe? Remembering how to #FloatToLive or avoid a rip current might save your life!
2) Do I know what the weather will be like? Check the coastal forecasts, an offshore wind can blow you into trouble
3) Do I have the right gear? Sunhat, sunglasses, suncream (SPF 30+) and plenty to drink are a must, and if you are planning to paddleboard, don't forget to wear your buoyancy aid and a leash

If you are planning your weekend adventures, it’s best to leave the beers at home!

As we head into #BeWaterAware Week, we're supporting our friends @NFCCFire to encourage everyone to stay safe in, on and around water.
If the warm weather is tempting you to head outdoors, whether or not you intend to enter the water, remembering two simple messages could save your life or someone else’s.
1. Cold water shock can kill, even on a warm day. If you find yourself struggling in the water. Remember to #FloatToLive…
- Don't panic
- Tilt your head back, with your ears under water
- Use your hands to stay afloat
- Control your breathing
- Then call for help
2. Know what to do in an emergency - #PhoneFloatThrow
- Call 999
- Encourage them to float on their back
- Throw something that floats - never enter the water yourself
#RespectTheWater and have a great day!
#StaySafe #ReadyForAnything
