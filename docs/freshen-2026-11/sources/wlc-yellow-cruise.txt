A stunning cruise along the southern half of Lake Windermere
The scenic cruise connects Lakeside Pier (the southernmost tip of Lake Windermere) with the bustling town of Bowness-on-Windermere half way up the lake. From the boat, you can see Lake District fells, historic houses and beautiful ancient woodlands.
Get off the boat at Lakeside for connections to the Lakeside & Haverthwaite Steam Railway , woodland walks and Lakes Aquarium .
Combined tickets are available.
Get off the boat at Bowness and you're right in the heart of this bustling Lakeland village, complete with many shops, cafes, bars and hotels.
More information:
Enjoy a 90 minute return cruise or get off at Lakeside and catch a later boat back.
Alternatively, start at Lakeside Pier, get off at Bowness and return on a later boat.
Each cruise includes a fascinating commentary on the history, attractions and points of interest surrounding Windermere.

Departure Points
Start this cruise from any of these locations
Bowness
As our busiest pier, Bowness offers the widest variety of cruises on the lake.
Located at postcode LA23 3HQ , it's a 2-minute walk from Bowness village and the closest pier to Windermere Village (10 minutes by bus/car or a 25-minute walk).
While we don't have our own parking, there are over 400 pay-and-display spaces within walking distance. You'll also find easy access to bus connections and national rail services via Windermere Railway Station.

Lakeside
Located at the southernmost tip of Windermere, Lakeside is our quietest pier, and home to some of the area's family-friendly attractions.
The pier's postcode is LA12 8AS and has over 300 on-site pay and display car parking spaces at a reasonable price.
It provides direct access to the Lakeside & Haverthwaite Railway (seasonal) , but bus services are limited with the nearest bus stop being at Newby Bridge (1.5 miles away via footpath).
