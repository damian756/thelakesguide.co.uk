Parking
The Brockhole on Windermere car park is open to the public between 9am and 6pm, these times are subject to seasonal changes so please check signage on-site before parking. Parking can be purchased from the on-site pay and display machines or via the Ring Go app.
​
Tarrifs:
​
Up to 2 hours - £5.50
3 hours - £6.50
4 hours - £7.00
5 hours - £7.50
All day until 6pm - £9.00
