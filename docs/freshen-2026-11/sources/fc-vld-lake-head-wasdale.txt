Lake Head Car Park, Wasdale
Type: Car Park
Wasdale Head , Cumbria , CA20 1EX
About
Pay & Display car park with 100 spaces.
Public Toilets adjacent to car park.
Located at the head of Wastwater, this is a popular pay-and-display car park for accessing the route to Scafell Pike and surrounding fells.
