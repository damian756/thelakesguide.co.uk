Flexible Dial-a-Ride Ulverston Local Service
You can travel between North Lonsdale Road and the Health Centre using the flexible Dial-a-Ride X70 service. The service only operates between Monday and Friday.
Book your journey with the operator at least 24 hours before travelling:
X70 Ulverston Local Service
Telephone: 01529 531 995 or 07875 559 118
