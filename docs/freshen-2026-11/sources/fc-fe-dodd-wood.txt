Parking and prices
Close
The car park is on a 'Pay on Exit' system. There is one pay station beside the toilet block and the car park is open 24 hours.
Charges
Up to 20 minutes: No charge
First hour: £2.10
Additional time £0.55 per 15 minutes
All day maximum charge: £13.00
Parking charges apply also to Blue Badge holders. The machines take credit and debit cards (contactless payment is available) or you have up to 48hrs after your visit to pay online at Park with Ease . If you arrive car-free there is no charge.
Visit often?  Members park for free. Buy  Dodd Wood membership  now for £30 to simply park and go while supporting your favourite forest. Or choose to buy a  Forestry England national membership  for £8 a month (£96 a year, annual contract applies) and enjoy visiting forests all over the country, including nearby Whinlatter Forest and Grizedale Forest. Membership covers the whole household.
Members: This site has barrierless ANPR. You do not need to display your membership car disc here, your number plate will be recognised. Please visit  Manage My Membership  if you need to update your vehicle registrations. Only one vehicle is permitted per visit. Free parking for Forestry England national members and Dodd Wood members only.

Dodd Wood
Discover amazing views of the northern Lake District, fantastic walks and rare wildlife
Dodd Wood is a fantastic place to enjoy some of the most stunning views of the northern Lake District. The network of walking trails will take you deep into the woodland and walk to the top of Dodd Summit for views across the fells.
You may also see gorgeous red squirrels in the wood throughout the year.

At a glance
Forest fire risk - Never light a BBQ, campfire or stove in the forest.

Still in the Spedding family, the house is delightfully unspoilt and harks back to the heyday of the English country manor house. Open from April to October tickets are available from the tearoom.
