Historic Hotel & Climbers' Bar in Great Langdale
Nestled in the stunning Great Langdale Valley, The Old Dungeon Ghyll is the original Dale Head Inn and a true centerpiece of the Lake District. Perfectly positioned amidst some of England's highest mountains, it has long been a haven for fell walkers and climbers seeking warm hospitality and breathtaking scenery. After a day on the fells, unwind with a pint of local ale and hearty, satisfying food in the iconic Climbers Bar. Experience the timeless charm of the Lake District at this historic inn.
Walk from the door to The Langdale Pikes, Jack's Rake, Bowfell and Crinkle Crags.
