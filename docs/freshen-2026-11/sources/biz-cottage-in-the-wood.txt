Welcome to the Lake District’s natural choice
On a winding mountain pass, deep in the Whinlatter Forest above Keswick in the northern Lake District, you’ll find The Cottage in the Wood, a unique Michelin Star Restaurant with Rooms by Jack & Beth Bond. We offer a tasting menu created with ingredients from local farms and sustainable suppliers. We have 8 boutique bedrooms available, so you can stay the night in our stunning, natural environment. Whether it’s for a couple of hours or couple of days, a warm welcome awaits you.

Michelin Star Dining
We work with local Cumbrian farmers, as well as the best of British suppliers who share our regenerative ethos. We’re lucky to based near the forest, fells and seashores that provide a rich larder for our Michelin Star Restaurant. Expect locally picked vegetables, grass fed beef from our Yorkshire neighbours, freshly caught fish from day boats, hand dived shellfish from British waters, and a host of foraged, wild foods.
