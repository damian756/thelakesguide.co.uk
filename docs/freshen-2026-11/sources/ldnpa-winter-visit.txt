Plan your winter visit to the Lake District
We have four seasons in one day, prepare for them all
This winter, we’re asking visitors to be prepared while enjoying the Lake District. Winter is challenging, tackling heights takes experience and skill, so it’s important to know your limits. On this page, you’ll find ways to make your visit memorable, while also keeping it a safe and beautiful place to be.
Understand and check the weather
Don’t get caught out by fading light and make sure you know when it will start to get dark. Check how long your walk will take and head out in good time. Remember, the Lake District’s deep valleys will get dark before the sunset.
Weather in the Lake District
The weather in the Lake District can vary a lot, not just between seasons but even within one day! Whether you’re heading out for a scenic walk or tackling a high fell, checking the latest forecast is essential. Our Lake District weather page has useful advice for understanding key winter weather terminology and frequently asked questions.
The Met Office Lake District mountain forecast provides an outlook for fell top weather conditions, warnings and hazards.
Lake District weather
Weatherline
Weatherline is the daily Helvellyn winter fell top weather conditions report from December to April every year, recorded by our Fell Top Assessors . Daily photos from Helvellyn show the varied conditions of the Lake District, highlighting the difference between valley bottom and fell summit.
Weatherline report
Arrive prepared and plan ahead
The English Lake District offers some of the best routes, lakes and villages in the world. Whether you are enjoying a day on the lake, a gentle low-level stroll or challenging mountain hike, conditions can change quickly at any time of year.
What to pack
Pack for the weather you’ll get, not the weather you want. Wearing the correct clothing and carrying the right gear is essential for a safe and enjoyable day. Our recommended kit list will help you stay prepared so you can stay safe and have fun.
See the checklist
Explore safely and know the local hazards
Whilst exploring the Lake District is generally safe, it does come with risks. Proper preparation reduces the chances of an incident, ensuring an enjoyable adventure. Check out our top tips, know what hazards you might experience and how to call for help in an emergency to help you stay safe on the trails.
Walking safety
Learn basic map reading
Mobile phones are essential for emergency situations, but they can run out of battery. A paper map will get you out of difficult situations if your phone has no signal or no battery, especially as taking the wrong turn in poor visibility winter weather can send you off route. Do you know how to read a map? Learn basic map skills with OS maps to help navigate around the Lake District.
Read a map with OS
Learn from an expert and explore well known routes
Each winter, our Fell Top Assessors walk up Helvellyn every day to check conditions, take photos and write a report. You can join our Fell Top Assessors for a day on a Winter Skills Day Course , to learn the basics of how to use an ice axe and crampons for winter walking.

A top tip from Fell Top Assessor, Jim
So, you’re heading up the high fells, but do you have the right gear and know how to use it? Watch this tutorial on how to use crampons in the snow. He explains the different techniques you can use, including flat footing, front pointing and a hybrid of both. If you want to learn more about using crampons and ice axes, book onto a Winter Skills Day Course.

Stay safe in and on the water
Swimming in tarns, lakes and rivers is great fun, and a wonderful way to experience the Lake District landscape. It’s always important to stay safe in and on the water, particularly in winter. Use the pages below to find information about swimming safely, the swim safe code and how to stay safe on the water in a boat, kayak or paddleboard all year round.
How to swim safely
Learn the Swim Safe Code, cold water shock and watch some video guides on how to be seen in the water.
Be safe on a boat
Read safety tips and watch Lake Ranger video guides for all boat users so you can enjoy your boating experience safely.
Paddleboard and kayak safety
Explore how to take to the water safely on a canoe, kayak or paddleboard with safety videos and some top tips.

Help us take care of the Lake District all year round
Whether you’re new to the area or a seasoned visitor, please be #LakeDistrictKind and help us look after the landscape you love.
1.
Leave no trace, please take your litter and dog poo home.
2.
Do not park where you are blocking gates or road access.
3.
Dogs must be on leads anywhere there may be livestock.
4.
Fly camping by the side of lakes and in laybys is not allowed.
5.
Wild camping is only allowed with the permission of the land owner.
6.
Fires and BBQs are not permitted in the Park unless stated on private land.
7.
Campervans and motorhomes should stay in designated sites.
