Leading Derwentwater Hotel - Lodore Falls Hotel & Spa
From lazy lakeside strolls to adventurous excursions and romantic weekends away, you can experience it all during your stay at the Lodore Falls. Dine on delicious menus, enjoy blissfully comfy bedrooms and feel pampered by the friendly team. Elegantly offering the perfect combination of romance, luxury and lakeside adventure - this Lake District Hotel & Spa radiates countryside sophistication. Complete with a doggie friendly space for cultured canines, every room in the Lodore Falls Hotel & Spa is stylish, homely and comfortably chic.

Step into something special with our one-of-a-kind Falls Cabana at the Lodore Falls Hotel & Spa – your private spot overlooking the stunning Lodore Falls, with breathtaking views across Derwentwater to Catbells and beyond. Award-Winning Dining
The Lodore Falls Hotel & Spa is an ideal destination for foodies with an array of excellent dining options. Boasting the Falls Brasserie, a restaurant with lake views serving decadent afternoon teas during the day and locally inspired culinary creations in an evening, and Mizu, a pan-Asian restaurant where you can experience exotic dishes that span the countries of the Far East.
Bar meals and light lunches are also available in the bar and lounge areas. Whatever you fancy, there’s always something tasty on the menu.

We are an environmentally conscious company with the aim of reducing our impact on the planet. We have a huge range of practices and iniatives for sustainability within our group of hotels, restaurants, spa, teams and suppliers.
