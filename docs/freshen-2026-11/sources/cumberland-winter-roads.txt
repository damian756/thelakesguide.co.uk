Winter road maintenance and gritting
How we grit and maintain roads in Cumberland during winter, including priority gritting routes, road conditions and salt bins.

Overview
Winter weather can affect roads across Cumberland. Between October and April, we provide a winter maintenance service to help keep key routes open during frost, ice and snow.
We cannot treat every road. We prioritise routes that are important for travel and access to essential services.
When cold, icy or snowy weather is forecast, we may send gritters out to spread salt on our priority gritting routes .
You can check where we are gritting, track our gritter lorries or report a problem with a road.

Gritting during winter
We use weather forecasts and road surface temperatures to decide when roads need treating. Gritting is usually carried out before frost or ice forms.
You should be aware that:
not all roads are included on our gritting routes
residential streets and many housing estate roads are not routinely treated
salt can be less effective on quieter roads because it needs traffic to help spread it
gritting does not guarantee that a road will be free from ice
Take extra care when travelling during winter weather, even if a road has been gritted.
Severe weather
During severe snow or prolonged winter weather, we may concentrate our resources on the highest priority routes.
Other routes will be treated when priority routes are in a safe condition. We may also need to temporarily close roads during extreme weather.
Salt bins
We may provide salt bins where there is an identified need following a site assessment.
Locations already covered by our gritting routes will not normally qualify for a salt bin unless there is a specific safety need.
Community support
We work with parish and town councils, community groups and volunteers to help communities during severe winter weather. Support includes:
the Snow Champions scheme, where volunteers help clear snow and support vulnerable residents
the Parish Salt Scheme, which provides additional salt or salt and grit mixtures to parish and town councils during extreme weather
support for community groups carrying out agreed winter maintenance on local routes
Contact us to ask about community winter support
Online form: Highways enquiry
Telephone: 0300 373 3736
Winter travel advice
Before travelling during severe winter weather:
check the weather forecast and road conditions
allow extra time for your journey
reduce your speed and allow more stopping distance
make sure your vehicle is prepared for winter conditions
consider whether you need to travel
Read our snow, ice and winter ready advice for more information.
Roads maintained by National Highways
National Highways maintains and grits the following major roads in Cumberland:
M6
A66
A595 from the A66 to Calder Bridge
A69 from M6 junction 43 at Carlisle, heading east
