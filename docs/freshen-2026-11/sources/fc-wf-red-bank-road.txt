Find a car park near you
Red Bank road car park
Open all day, seven days a week, chargeable times 8am to 8pm. Pay by Phone code: 9919.
Available parking
General: 77
Disabled spaces: 6
Motorcycle parking: can also use any spaces, with consideration to other vehicles
There are no height restrictions.
Length of stay
Short and long stay parking.
Charges
Motorcycles can park for free.
Charges per hour:
Up to 1 hours: £2.60
Up to 2 hours: £4.40
Up to 3 hours: £5.80
Up to 4 hours: £7.40
Up to 6 hours: £8.80
All day: £10.60
Blue Badge holders
You can park one hour for free on top of time purchased when displaying a valid Blue Badge and clock disc.
Payment options
You cannot pay with cash at this car park.
You can pay by:
card: pay with Visa and MasterCard debit and credit cards, as well as contactless card payments
phone - the location code for this car park is: 9919
Find out how to  register to pay by phone.
Find information on how to avoid parking scams
3 and 7 day parking pass
You can  buy a 3 or 7 day parking pass for this car park.
Parking permit
You can  apply for a parking permit for this car park.
If you have a problem with this car park
If you have problems paying due to an issue with the ticket machine you can let us know by  reporting a problem with this car park .
