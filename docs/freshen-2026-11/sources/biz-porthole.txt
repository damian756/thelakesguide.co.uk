Welcome to Porto
An independent family-run fine dining restaurant offering some of the best food in Bowness-on-Windermere.
A popular and vibrant restaurant with an easy-going vibe, Porto offers an exciting modern dining experience. Choose a seat in the cosy downstairs area, upstairs in our relaxed restaurant, or when its sunny our rooftop terrace is always open.
Expect a friendly welcome, stylish surroundings and fresh, full of flavour good food at Porto.
In a prime location
On a cosmopolitan cobbled street in the heart of the tourist town of Bowness-on-Windermere
In a prime location on a cosmopolitan cobbled street in the heart of the tourist town of Bowness-on-Windermere, Porto is just a short 5-minute stroll from Windermere, England’s longest lake. Sail around the lake’s many islands before sharing some small plates and cocktails on our rooftop sun terrace.
For locals and tourists alike, Porto is a must-visit if you want consistently good food and drink and a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
