Winter Times
9 Nov 2026 – 19 Mar 2027
Our Derwentwater lake cruises operate both clockwise and anti-clockwise around the lake. There are occasions when the weather conditions prohibit sailings. Online booking not available at present but you can still buy tickets at our ticket office by the lake at the Keswick Boat Landings.
