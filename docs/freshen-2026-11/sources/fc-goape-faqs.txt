Health, Safety & Accessibility
Can I do Go Ape if I'm pregnant? For the safety of both mother and child, Go Ape strongly recommends that pregnant women do not take part in our adventures.
Our adventures are robust physical activities involving climbing, balancing, and zipping, which naturally carry a degree of risk. While we don't want to be discriminatory, we are not medical experts.
We strongly encourage you to seek advice from your doctor before deciding to participate.
If, after consulting your doctor, you choose to proceed, we will require you to formally declare your pregnancy to a staff member and sign a specific disclaimer acknowledging that:
"I have made Go Ape aware that I am pregnant and, as a result, Go Ape has recommended that I do not take part on the course. However, I have decided to take part on the course in the full knowledge that this may incur risks to my unborn child."
We want you to have a safe and wonderful experience, so please prioritise medical advice when making this decision!
Do I need to be fit and strong? Go Ape designs its adventures to be as accessible as possible. Our high ropes courses require a degree of physical fitness but if you can climb up a rope ladder you will be fine. Our Forest Segways require you to stand for an hour on a moving platform and require similar energy as riding a bicycle. Nets courses have a lot of bounce and therefore require similar energy to a bouncy castle.
Are carer's tickets/discounts available for Go Ape adventures? Yes, carers' tickets/discounts are available for Go Ape adventures.
If a supervising adult is not required to participate as per our activity restrictions, please call our customer service team on 01603 895500 to discuss your requirements and book your free carer ticket.
If a supervising adult is required to participate under our activity restrictions, please call us on 01603 895500 , and we will apply an accessibility discount of 10% for you.
Can I take part if I have an injury or medical condition? Go Ape activities are physically and emotionally challenging outdoor adventures that come with a risk of injury when participating.
If you or anyone you are supervising has a pre-existing injury or medical condition (including pregnancy) that could affect your ability to participate safely or may increase your risk of injury, we would recommend that you do not take part in the activity.
However, we do not want to be discriminatory and ultimately the choice is yours. We are not medical experts and therefore suggest that you seek advice from your doctor on the matter.
If you decide to proceed, you will need to declare that you have an existing injury. In addition to your risk acknowledgement form, you will be asked to complete an additional form on the day of your adventure stating that you have decided to take part in the activity in the full knowledge that this may incur additional risks to your existing injury.
Please make Go Ape staff aware on arrival and they will provide you with the additional form. If you would like to discuss anything with us before you decide, please reach out via email to questions@goape.co.uk or give us a call on 01603 895500 .
I might need additional support At Go Ape, we strive to welcome everyone, but our courses do require a degree of physical and mental challenge.
For some, this may mean we need to arrange additional equipment, adjust our training, or provide higher levels of supervision to ensure your safe participation. In certain instances, the inherent risks may still be too great, even with these measures, preventing the use of the course.
The best next step is to call us!
Please contact our booking team on 01603 895500 to discuss your specific requirements before you book. This allows us to ensure we can meet your needs so you have the safest and best experience possible. We can also suggest booking during a quieter time of day to avoid unnecessary pressure from other participants.
We're here to help you plan your adventure!
What if I have a pre-existing medical condition? Our staff at Go Ape are awesome, but not medically qualified, therefore cannot assess the capacity of participants to undertake a course. It remains your responsibility to undertake such an assessment prior to use of the course. We advise you to visit the course prior to booking to undertake your own risk assessment.
Will I be wearing a helmet? You will only have to wear a helmet if you're participating on Forest Segway. Go Ape has consulted widely whilst risk assessing the benefits or disadvantages of participants wearing helmets on our high ropes experiences. We have concluded that the disadvantages of participants wearing helmets outweigh the minimal benefits, and the Health and Safety authorities have agreed with our approach.
Groups, Schools & Corporate
What happens if I want to change the number of participants in my group booking? Amendments can be made up to 28-days before the event date. If you're within 28-days of your event date, amendments are no longer permitted. Please refer to the full Corporate Events Team, terms and policies .
Can I amend my group event booking? Amendments can be made up to 28-days before your event date, just call your dedicated Events Coordinator on their direct line stated on your quote. Please note: if you're within 28-days to your event date, amendments are no longer permitted.
Do we need to sign risk acknowledgement forms for a school trip? Yes, you'll need to complete risk acknowledgement forms for your group before your arrival on site. Completing the forms prior will ensure a quick start on the day.
Please note that the supervising adults visiting on the day will need to complete the risk acknowledgement forms and not the parents. Please only sign for children you are responsible for on the day and adhere to the supervision guidelines for your activity/age range as this will vary.
You can find all of our risk assessments on our terms and policies page . It will vary per activity. Speak to your education executive for more information.
Do you offer a group discount? We do offer group discounts, enquire with us to find out more.
Do you require a deposit for event bookings? A 25% deposit is required to reserve your adventure, if the booking date is more than 28-days away. Full payment is then required 14-days prior to event date.
How do I book a group? We have a dedicated events team who are there to help with larger groups such as school trips or company team building. Please visit our Groups page to book.
How much do group visits cost? Prices vary depending on your group day out requirements. Send us an enquiry to get a quote. We have exclusive prices for school groups during term-time Monday to Friday, which we can talk you through as well.
How much does a school trip cost? School trips start from £15 per pupil, with required supervisors going free. School rates apply Monday to Friday during term time. Contact us via our schools page , and we'd be delighted to send you a bespoke quote for your requirements.
Is there a minimum or maximum group number for a team day? We can cater for any number from 10 - 300 participants! Just visit our team days page for more information.
What's included in a school trip to Go Ape? Every school trip is different and tailored to meet your learning objectives. However, our most popular school trip includes a tree top experience, ground-based games and a bring your own lunch.
Visit our schools page to request a quote and speak to a member of the education team to build a bespoke package for your school.
When is full payment due for an event booking? Full payment is due 14-days prior to your event date.
Can you do team building for 100+ people? We can host team building for groups of 100 plus people. Large groups are split into activity rotations: high ropes and other activities such as Axe Throwing, Archery Tag and team building games run simultaneously. Dedicated event coordinators manage logistics.
Is Go Ape a good Christmas team building activity? Yes, winter activities include high ropes under the canopy, competitive Axe Throwing and some seasonal activities like Sundown Adventures in autumn. Book early as select locations close over winter and December can get booked up fast.
Do you have a recommended hotel partner? Go Ape has teamed up with Village Hotels to offer the perfect base for your visit. Conveniently located near many of our adventure locations, Village Hotels combine comfortable rooms with great leisure facilities, making them an easy choice for groups and overnight stays.
Do you help with accommodation for away days or overnight stays? Yes — planning an away day with an overnight stay? Our team will help find accommodation options nearby across all our locations. Call 01284 852210.
Parties & Celebrations
Can I book a birthday party? Of course! We would love to help you celebrate. You can book your party activity online. There's even an option to add party bags which will be ready for you to collect on arrival or when you finish your adventure. If there are not enough spaces available in one session, please book more spaces in the subsequent session and you will then be able to go round the course together.
All of our locations have picnic benches around the forest or somewhere you can put a blanket to enjoy a birthday cake or a picnic with your group. Some of our locations have a forest shelter or party room which you can hire by calling 01603 895500 .
Can I book a Forest Shelter? Outdoor shelters are available to book by calling 01603 895500 .
Do you have party invites? Yes! We can provide a PDF for you to download and print.
How do I book a birthday party online? You can book your party via the "book now" function on our website as you would with a regular booking. You will get the option to add party bags before you get to the checkout. To add a forest shelter, please call our booking line team at 01603 895500 or email us at questions@goape.co.uk .
How long is my party booking for? If you are booking Treetop Adventure or Treetop Adventure Plus your party will be in the trees for 1 hour, but we recommend allowing up to 2 hours to include the harnessing, safety brief and certificate collection. If you have a Nets Adventure booking then you will be on the Nets for 2 hours.
How many people can I invite to a birthday party? We don't have a minimum or maximum number of people you can invite for a birthday party! It mostly depends on the capacity of the timeslots you wish to book and the availability of your chosen site. If your group size is larger than the capacity of your chosen timeslot, please send us an email at questions@goape.co.uk with your enquiry and our customer care team can look into this for you.
Is parking included in birthday party bookings? Parking fees are not included in birthday party bookings as the parking is determined by the landowner of the site, not Go Ape.
What if the weather is bad, will my party still go ahead? Go Ape is an excellent wet weather activity. Our courses are substantially sheltered from rain and wind by the trees themselves so as long as you're appropriately dressed for the weather you should still have a great day. The course remains open in all weathers, except thick ice, thunderstorms and gales. Rain makes the course muddier than usual which can add to the fun, but you might want dry clothes to change into for the journey home.
If we have to close for unsafe weather conditions, we will contact you to reschedule your adventure or offer a refund. If the weather is unsafe on the day we will update social media at the earliest opportunity to try and inform people before they leave home. We will also set about calling every customer with a booking, so please leave your contact number when making a new booking.
What supervision is required for a birthday party? Supervision rules and participant restrictions vary depending on the adventure you choose and the Go Ape location. Check out our?Locations?page and click through to the site you wish to visit. Then when you click through on your chosen adventure you'll find the information you need.
What's included in a team day at Go Ape? Every team day at Go Ape is different and tailored to meet the needs of your group. Our most popular team days consist of a tree top experience, preferential parking and a dedicated host. You can also add your choice of catering options, team development activities and more to create your forest VIP experience. Find out more on our team days page .
Booking & Managing Your Adventure
Can I supervise children on different high ropes activities at the same time? Adults (18+) can simultaneously supervise children participating in Treetop Adventure and Treetop Adventure Plus from the ground.
Adults (18+) cannot simultaneously supervise children participating in Treetop Challenge and other high ropes activities such as Treetop Adventure and Treetop Adventure Plus.
Adults participating in an activity may only supervise children on the same activity.
Please make sure that there are enough adults (18+) in your group to supervise the children (under 16 years) you have booked for, meeting the supervision requirements for your activity. This includes adults participating in some instances, and they also require a ticket.
Do I need to book in advance? We strongly recommend booking your adventure online or by phone before travelling.
Go Ape sessions often sell out, especially during weekends and peak season. If you wait until you arrive, we cannot guarantee there will be any spaces left, and we genuinely hate sending people away disappointed! Book ahead to secure your spot.
How can I book? You can book online . This is the fastest way to check availability and guarantee your spot!
If you prefer to talk it through, call us at 01603 895500 .
Booking for a group? For schools, company outings and more, please check out our Groups pages . Our dedicated events team will help organise all the details.
Moving bookings, cancellations and refunds If you need to move or cancel your booking, please call us on 01603 895500 . You can also move your booking using your online account up to 48 hours before your session.
Up to 7 days before your activity, you can cancel an existing booking and receive a full refund, minus the booking fee*. You can also move your booking free of charge.
Up to 48 hours before your activity, you can move the activity date, subject to availability. This will incur a £5 administration charge. A refund is not possible within the 7-day period before your activity date.
Within 48 hours of your activity date, if Go Ape are operational, unfortunately you will not be entitled to move your booking, or receive any refunds for cancellations. Please call us if you can longer attend so we can let the team at site know.
*If you originally moved your booking within the 7-day period, you will not be eligible for a refund on the moved booking. If your booking is made less that 7 days before your activity date, it is non- refundable.
See our full booking terms and conditions .
Where is my nearest Go Ape? With 36 sites across the UK there's bound to be a Go Ape site within reach.
Head to our Locations page to explore our courses or find your nearest site by searching using your postcode.
Can I turn up and book at the site? Yes, you can book at the Go Ape cabin on the day, but availability isn’t guaranteed. Sessions often fill up quickly, especially at weekends and during holidays, so we recommend booking online in advance to secure your spot.
Can I manage my booking online? If you need to reschedule your Go Ape adventure, you can do this using your online account up to 48 hours before your session. Can groups of different ages participate together at Go Ape? Yes, mixed groups of different ages can participate together at Go Ape. Our courses are designed to accommodate a wide range of ages, allowing families and friends of all ages to enjoy the experience together.
Do all of your sites have a forest shelter available? The following sites have a forest shelter available (please note our Battersea Park site has a party room): Alice Holt, Bedgebury, Black Park, Bracknell, Buxton, Cannock Chase, Cockfosters, Coventry, Crawley, Dalby, Dalkeith Edinburgh, Delamere, Forest of Dean (Mallards Pike), Haldon Forest, Leeds Castle, Matfen, Moors Valley, Rivington, Sherwood Pines, Southampton, Thetford Forest, Wendover Woods, Whinlatter, Woburn, Wyre Forest
Do supervising adults need to buy a ticket or book a spot? To supervise from the ground you don't need to buy a ticket or book. Some of our activities require you to be on the course too. In this case you will need to buy a ticket. Supervision requirements can be found on the location and activity pages of the website.
We recommend participating with your children so you can enjoy your adventure together, especially if it's their first time or they are feeling a little nervous.
How do I change the date of my booking? You can change the date of your booking up to 48 hours before your session using your online account or by calling 01603 895500 .
If you didn't create an account, you can still create one now using the same email address that you used to make your booking.
Please note that there is a £5 fee for rescheduling a booking within 7 days of your session. Bookings closer than 48 hours cannot be rescheduled.

What if I can't see any availability for our chosen date? We may still be able to accommodate you. For example school trips, team days and groups bookings can often be made on days when we're not usually open. Please get in touch and our team will be happy to help.
What is the difference between the recommended age and the minimum age for an activity? Minimum Age: This is the absolute youngest age that participants must be to take part in a specific Go Ape activity. It's a mandatory requirement set for safety reasons.
Recommended Age: This suggests the age at which we believe most participants will have the best experience, based on physical ability, maturity, and the challenge level of the course. The recommended age is not mandatory but serves as guidance to ensure participants are fully prepared for the adventure.
Why does Go Ape charge a booking fee? We charge a booking fee to cover the costs associated with processing bookings efficiently and securely. This non-refundable fee covers various operational expenses, including website maintenance, hosting fees, payment processing fees, customer support services, and technology investments aimed at improving your booking experience.
Why does the price of the high ropes activities change by day? The pricing for our high ropes activities fluctuates based on demand, which is a common practice in the leisure industry. Quieter days are offered at lower prices, while peak days have higher pricing to reflect increased demand.
Why is it cheaper to book online? Booking online is more cost effective due to automated processes, so less involvement from our team.
Our friendly customer care team is still ready to help answer any questions you have, and can be reached by email at questions@goape.co.uk or by phone on 01603 895500 .
Over all, booking online means a smoother and more convenient experience for our customers.
How do I book Potion Hunt? Our brand-new family Halloween event can be booked over half term on Treetop Adventure, Treetop Adventure Plus and Nets Adventure from 24th October to 1st November (available at Dalkeith from 10th October and Sherwood Pines, Cannock Chase, Coventry and Leeds Castle from 17th October). Book your adventure as usual, then add for £5 per person at checkout. During the experience, solve clues to find ingredients that fit the potion recipe on your certificate and earn your prize including a medal (these may vary depending on availability).
On the Day & What to Bring
Can I bring a camera? Please do! You're going to want to capture these moments to share. We do ask that phones taken to height are attached to you to minimise the risk of dropping them. Our courses pass over paths and playgrounds so it's important that we are thinking of others. Phone pouches with a lanyard are available to buy from our cabins for £6, but used at your own risk.
If you'd like to take a camera, please discuss with your instructor on arrival to make sure it is not too bulky and can be safely attached. Go Ape will not be responsible for any damage sustained to personal equipment.
Can I bring my dog? Most Go Ape locations are dog-friendly, making them a great day out for both you and your four-legged friend. We recommend checking the landlord’s website before visiting for any local restrictions or guidance. Please note that dogs are not permitted at our Woburn Safari Park location.
Dogs must be kept on a lead and under control near our courses. If a dog becomes disruptive or noisy, we may ask owners to move away from the area to ensure everyone enjoys their visit. Please be considerate — not all guests are comfortable around dogs — and always clean up after your pet, using the bins provided by our landlords.
While we love dogs, they can’t join you on the activities or be left unattended while you take part. Please make sure you arrange suitable supervision if you’re going up into the trees or heading out on a Segway.
Nets Adventure: Well-behaved dogs on leads are welcome in the Nets Adventure seating area with supervising adults. This is an enclosed space, so please be mindful that not everyone may be comfortable around dogs.
Can I wear a bumbag or backpack? Bumbags and backpacks aren't permitted on the course as they may increase your risk of injury or get in the way of your harness. A lanyard and phone pouch can be purchased online at the time of booking or on the day from the Go Ape cabin (subject to availability). If you need to carry any medication with you, please let a member of staff know who will be able to provide you with a pouch which can be attached to your harness.
We recommend that non-essential items and bags are left somewhere safe such as in your car. Small items such as car keys can be left in the Go Ape cabin.
What happens in bad weather? Go Ape is an excellent wet weather activity. Our courses are substantially sheltered from rain and wind by the trees themselves so as long as you are appropriately dressed for the weather you should still have great memories of your experience. The course remains open in all weathers, except thick ice, thunderstorms and gales. Rain makes the course muddier than usual which can add to the fun, but you might want dry clothes to change into for the journey home.
If we have to close for unsafe weather conditions, we will offer you a refund, credit note or reschedule your booking. If unsafe weather is forecasted we will aim to make the decision as early as possible, and will do our best to contact you to try and save you a wasted journey.
What if I am running late for my Go Ape session? We highly recommend arriving 20 minutes before your scheduled booking time. This gives you plenty of time to park, use the facilities, and get checked in at the Go Ape cabin so you're ready to go when your session starts.
We understand that sometimes things happen on the way, and you might find yourself running a bit behind. Unfortunately, we're unable to make any changes to your booking on the day itself. Our sessions run on a very tight schedule to ensure everyone gets their full adventure, and once a session has started, it's really difficult to slot people in.
If you are running late, please head straight to the Go Ape cabin as soon as you arrive. Our staff on site will be the best people to speak with. They'll assess the situation and do their absolute best to see if there's any way to accommodate you, given the current session timings and availability. However, there's no guarantee as we run a very tight ship with our session timings.
What if I'm afraid of heights! A fear of heights is both common and natural. Go Ape can help you overcome your lifelong fear. We are proud to have helped hundreds of thousands of people conquer their fear of heights.
We recommend you book at quieter times when we can offer you more support, and when fewer people will be on the course with you. Let your instructor know and they will be able to give you more attention. Why not give it a try - you will surprise yourself!
What should I wear? Dress for the weather to stay warm and dry in colder weather, and sunscreen and a sunhat if it's sunny.
Closed-toe and closed-heel shoes are essential (trainers recommended). No sandals.
No bare waists for harness safety and comfort.
Gloves are recommended (available for purchase at the cabin).
Phone pouches are required if you wish to take photos of your adventure (available for purchase at the cabin).
For Nets Adventure we recommend wearing a long sleeve top and trousers.
When do I need to arrive? Please allow plenty of time to park your car and use the toilets (as usage restrictions may be in place). You should aim to be at the cabin 20 minutes before your booked time. We will be operating a card only policy at the Go Ape cabin but some parking facilities may still be taking cash so do make sure that you bring some with you.
Can I bring or hire a bike? Most Go Ape locations are fantastic places to enjoy a cycle, and many also offer bike hire. At Alice Holt and Black Park, the cycle hire is called Forest Biking and is operated by Go Ape. You can also reserve bike and cycle extras at these locations when you book your adventure.
Go Ape locations including Bracknell, Dalby Forest, Glentress Forest, Grizedale and Haldon Forest are well known cycling destinations. They feature a mixture of family cycling trails and more extreme routes for experienced mountain bikers. More information about local routes, cycling restrictions and cycle hire not operated by Go Ape can be found on our landlords' websites.
Please note that Go Ape cannot take responsibility for your bike whilst your participate in our activities.
Can I keep items in my pockets on the high ropes course? Due to the nature of our treetop activities, we strongly recommend that pockets must be emptied to prevent the risk of dropping any personal belongings while on the course. Some courses are built over a mixture of vegetation, streams, and in some locations even lakes and ponds. There is therefore a risk of personal loss if items are dropped into areas that cannot safely be reached by foot.
Can I wear a Burka at Go Ape? At Go Ape, your safety and comfort are our top priorities. We recommend wearing comfortable, movable clothing that you don't mind getting a bit dirty.
You can absolutely wear a Burka at Go Ape, as long as it allows for the safe fitting and operation of our harnesses. Our harnesses are designed to fit snugly over your clothing, and any loose or flowing fabric could potentially get caught in equipment or hinder your movement.
We do ask that you ensure your clothing:
Allows for a secure and comfortable harness fit. The harness needs to sit directly against your clothes to be effective.
Does not have loose elements that could snag or get tangled.
Allows for full freedom of movement as you'll be climbing, swinging, and zipping through the trees.
If you have any concerns about how your attire will work with our safety equipment, please get in touch at questions@goape.co.uk and our friendly staff will be able to advise you further and ensure you have the safest and most enjoyable experience possible.
Can spectators watch and take pictures from the forest floor? Absolutely! Spectators are more than welcome to come along and watch the action from the ground. Most of our courses are set in open woodland, with walking trails and viewing areas, so friends and family can follow along and cheer you on from below.
The only adventure with restrictions is Woburn Safari Park, which allows just one onlooker free of charge (additional spectators can be added to your booking at Woburn for an additional fee).
Do I need a head torch for a Sundown Adventure or Treetop Dates? A torch will be helpful for making your way from the car park to the cabin and for making your way to the toilets. It does not need to be a head torch, the torch on your phone will suffice. You will not be required to use it on the course.
It's going to be very warm on the day of my visit. How should I prepare? We have produced this handy guide on staying safe at Go Ape in hot weather . Please have a read through to prepare for your adventure.
Should I eat before my adventure? Our activities are robust and physically demanding, so please ensure you have had enough food and drink to fuel your adventure.
Where can I sign my risk acknowledgement form? Once you have made a booking you will be sent an email with a personalised link to sign your online risk acknowledgement form. If somebody else booked for you, please ask them to pass along the link.

Participation, Supervision & Age Requirements
Do you have a minimum/maximum weight and age for riding Segways? All participants must weigh a minimum of 7 stone or 45kg. This is to enable the participant to be able to safely operate the Segway which is controlled through shifting your body weight. Anyone who does not meet this requirement may not be able to safely stop the machine which would put themselves and others at risk. The maximum weight is 18.4 stone or 117kg.
