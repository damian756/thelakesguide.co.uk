SAME HOUSE · NEW CHAPTER
A new chapter at Barrow House
A historic Lake District house for group stays, gatherings and shared adventures.
Formerly Derwentwater Independent Hostel, this much-loved house in Borrowdale is beginning a new chapter as Barrow House Derwentwater.
Our family is bringing the place back to life, preparing to welcome guests in 2027 with dorm beds, private rooms, camping pods and opportunities to gather as a group.
We're carrying forward its charitable heart: creating a place where people from all walks of life can stay, gather, explore and experience this extraordinary landscape together.

LAKE · WOODLAND · WATERFALL
Gather your people and make the place your own.
Barrow House is made for groups - big shared spaces inside and out, bedrooms for everyone under one roof, and the lake, woodland and fells waiting beyond the door.
Weddings and celebrations, school adventures, church weekends, retreats, reunions and big family gatherings. However you fill the house, it's all yours.
The main house sleeps 63+ across 11 bedrooms. Our 3 heated camping pods in the grounds accommodate a further 4 per pod. That's 75+, not including the opportunity to pitch tents too.
