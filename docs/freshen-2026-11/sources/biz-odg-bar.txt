The Famous Climbers Bar, Great Langdale
The Bar
You'd be forgiven for calling this the "Hikers Bar".
​
The sign above the door said this for so many years. But this has always been the historic Great Langdale climbers' pub.
​
This part of the building was formerly a shippon, a cow shed. You can still see the bays to the right of the bar.
The shippon was converted to the bar in 1949.
​
The fire place was added much later by Jane and Neil Walmsley.
​
The ODG is well known for providing a good selection of local beers, whatever your choice you'll always be sure it's a good pint.
​
CAMRA member? Make sure to ask for your discount.
From the kitchen
The ODG has always prided itself on its fresh, home cooked food.
Our Head Chef Josh, and his team, Mark, Louis and Johnny create seasonal dishes fresh to order.
Try our very own, very popular sticky toffee pudding.
Or call in pre walk and take a "dead fly pie" for the fell.
​
Our specials and snacks are very popular - check the board for today's specials.
​
Food is served daily from 12.30pm to 8pm
​
Legends
Amongst many of the features of The ODG, one of the most recognisable is hung on the wall opposite the bar.
"Black Jack" Thornton was a technical college lecturer, accomplished climber and general party animal.
He and his gang took over the barn at Wall End Farm for their get togethers.
His picture depicts cherubs, beer, tents, Middle Fell, The ODG and a fair maiden awaiting his return.
Grab yourself a pint and see what else you can find in this wonderful art work by Shirley Parfitt.
