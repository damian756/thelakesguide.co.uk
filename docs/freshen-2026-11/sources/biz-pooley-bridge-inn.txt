Welcome to the Lake District
This wonderful inn is located in the centre of the pretty little village of Pooley Bridge only moments away from Ullswater. Our spacious, traditional bar features open fires, free wifi and a relaxing ambiance which welcomes both dogs and children. On better days, enjoy our pleasant outside tables both in our beautiful front garden and the peace of the Stable Courtyard away from the village bustle. Call us or pop in and say hello!!
Rooms
Very comfortable & affordable
We have 12 bedrooms that are all en-suite some with their own balcony to relax on. Overnight guests can start the day with a full English breakfast and all the trimmings – or partake in a lighter continental-style alternative. We have a couple of family rooms and some with adjoining doors. No matter what size of group we can accommodate you. Give us a call or simply book online with our discount code.

Food
Traditional Pub Food You'll Love
The Pooley Bridge Inn has something for everyone. A great selection of drinks  Traditional home cook food , Roast dinners , plenty of vegan and gluten free choices. Looking for something a little special our Seasonal Specials Menu offer the freshest locally sourced ingredients on every dish really showing off the best of Cumbrian and Scottish food.
