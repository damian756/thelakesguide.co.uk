Stay with us
After a day exploring the lakes, our range of accommodation offers a well-earned place to relax and recharge. The hotel has 11 comfortable bedrooms in the main building, plus three superior apartments and six self-catering apartments in our converted Lakeland barn. We also offer a well-kept campsite, as well as Lingmell House, our sister B&B, just a short walk from the Inn.
