Welcome To Fellpack
At Fellpack, we believe your experiences should be celebrated. Our boutique hotels in the Lake District are places to unwind, inspire exploration and toast to your days on the fells.
Situated on the flanks of Skiddaw and in the heart of vibrant Keswick, expect beautiful rooms and suites, dazzling views and award-winning dining that’s worth savouring.
The perfect base for excursions of all kinds, find your Fellpack and begin.

Our purpose is simple. We celebrate Lake District adventures. It all started in June 2017 with a small restaurant on Lake Road in Keswick. As we’ve grown, opened new doors and welcomed new faces to the team, that same passion continues to unite us all.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where are your hotels located?
Fellpack Skiddaw sits on the lower slopes of Skiddaw in a hamlet called Underskiddaw, close to Keswick. Fellpack Keswick is located on The Heads, in Keswick town. Both offer excellent bases for Lakeland adventures.
How many bedrooms are there at Fellpack Hotels?
You’ll find 25 bedrooms and suites at Fellpack Skiddaw and 11 bedrooms and suites at Fellpack Keswick.
Do your hotels have restaurants?
Fellpack Skiddaw is home to the award-winning Fellpack Restaurant. The Round and Eight Pizza are based in Keswick, a stone’s throw from Fellpack Keswick.
Do I need to book at the restaurants?
We advise booking at Fellpack Restaurant. The Round and Eight Pizza are walk-in only, but our tables turn over quickly, so we’ll always be able to fit you in.
What time do you serve food?
At Fellpack Restaurant, food is served from 12 pm - 9 pm all week.
At The Round, food is served from 3 pm - 9 pm Monday to Friday, 12 pm - 9 pm Saturday and 12 pm - 6 pm Sunday.
At Eight Pizza, food is served from 12 pm - 9 pm all week.
Is there a spa?
We don’t have a spa at our hotels, but we’ve partnered with The Spa at Underscar to offer access to its luxury spa and leisure facilities, the perfect way to wind down after a day exploring.
Are your Lake District hotels dog-friendly?
We don’t allow dogs at either of our Lake District hotels.
