105 between Greystoke and Penrith

Stagecoach routes
Stagecoach services include the number:
104 between Whinfell Forest Centre Parks and Carlisle
508 between Patterdale (Windermere in Summer) and Penrith
509 between Keswick and Penrith / Hartsop (Summer only)
563 between Penrith and Kirkby Stephen
UB1 between Aira Force and Howtown (Summer only)
UB2 between Aira Force and Lowther Castle (Summer only)
X4 and X5 between Penrith and Workington, via Keswick
Other routes
Little White Bus operates the number 113 between Garsdale and Gayle.
Telford Coaches operates the number 680A between Nenthead and Carlisle.
Go North East operates the number 681 between Alston and Haltwhistle and 691 between Hexham and Alston.
Wright Bros operate bus numbers 888, 888S and 889 between Nenthead, Hexham and Penrith.
Cumbria Transport Services operates the number 561 between Appleby and Kendal.
Western Dales operates the numbers S1 and S5 between Kirkby Stephen and Kendal.
