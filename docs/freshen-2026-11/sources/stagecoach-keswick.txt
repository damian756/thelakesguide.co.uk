Keswick Buses - Explore the area during your visit
There's so much to see in this welcoming market town. With its incredible mountain backdrop and Derwentwater, Keswick is the gateway for walking, sailing and scenery. Explore Derwentwater on the  Keswick Launch , or enjoy the stunning lake views from the National Trust's Friar's Crag walk.
A lively town with a plentiful selection of outdoor equipment shops and a very popular Saturday market, it boasts three parks and two museums.
