Welcome to Bryson's - Craft bakers of the Lake District
Located at the heart of the Lake District, a visit to Bryson’s of Keswick is essential for any trip to the Lakes. We have been providing our tasty treats and delicious bakes for over 70 years, and cater to all tastes including vegan and dairy free. Our flavourful products are made fresh in our Keswick bakery using locally-sourced ingredients, with no non-hydrogenated fats in any of our products.
You'll be bound to find your new favourite treat in our extensive baked range which includes artisan bread, cakes, cream cakes, savouries, and our renowned Lakeland Plumbread. If you're in Keswick or Bowness on Windermere, pop by and visit our bakery shops and tea rooms. If you popping by Cockermouth, Appleby, or Kirkby Stephen, a visit to our shops is a must.

Explore Our Tea Rooms
Visit our Tea Rooms
Bowness Tea Room
Don't miss out on our traditional tea room during your visit to Bowness-on-Windermere. Indulge in a delightful array of treats, including our authentic cream tea. Make your Lake District visit truly memorable with Bryson's culinary delights, located in Royal Square, Windermere.
Keswick Tea Room
Visit our original Keswick shop and tea room where the magic began over 70 years ago. Here, we began our tradition of hand-baking bread using locally milled flour. Nestled in the heart of Keswick, our shop and tea room offers a taste of history and local culinary artistry.
