Lake District, Cumbria Blog
Visit Lake District and Cumbria
The Official Guide to the region
With stunning mountain views, lovely beaches and shimmering lakes galore, summer is a great time of year to visit the Lake District, Cumbria. Enjoy captivating exhibitions at a great range of museums and art galleries and choose from a vibrant array of events at our brilliant selection of theatres and cinemas.  You’ll find ancient castles, historic ruins, old burial sites and stone circles dotted across the region.
You won’t be short on things to do – and why not keep things simple and explore car free. Hop between towns and the city by bus or train – along the way taking in beautiful coastal and mountain views!
With so much to do, why not stay a few days? We have plenty of superb accommodation options suitable for all – from luxury hotels to accessible glamping pods, there’s plenty to choose from.  Book your stay now  and get ready for some wonderful days away!
Visit our Autumn page to find out more.
You may leave the Lake District & Cumbria, but once you’ve been, it’ll never leave you.

Spring
The flowers are in bloom, the woods are a carpet of bluebells, wood sorrel and wild garlic. The new leaves shine like emeralds and the sheep have been brought down from the fells to lamb and graze in the warmer, low-lying pastures.
Summer
A Cumbrian summer is when England’s most alluring corner revels in its beauty. It’s time for a ramble across the fells, the clear skies making for the most stunning views. A day on a lake, soaking up the soft waves and glorious summer rays.
Autumn
Autumn in Cumbria sees the county undergo a beautiful change in scenery. The sweeping valleys turn to shades of orange, yellow and gold as autumn begins to take hold.
Winter
From guided alpaca walks to artisanal workshops on everything from glass cutting to boat building, a Cumbrian winter is one long festival of activities.
