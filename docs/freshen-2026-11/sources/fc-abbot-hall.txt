What's On
Abbot Hall is an art gallery in Kendal, home to one of the North’s most significant art collections. Set within a Grade I listed Georgian building on the banks of the River Kent, the gallery brings together historic and contemporary art through changing exhibitions, creative experiences and nationally important collections.
The collection includes watercolours by JMW Turner and John Ruskin alongside contemporary works by artists including Charmaine Watkiss, Paula Rego, Lela Harris and Bridget Riley.
Today, Abbot Hall is being reimagined as a vibrant cultural and community hub, with a programme exploring identity, landscape, ecology and lived experience. Through exhibitions, events, family activities and outreach, we connect people with the rich history and culture of Cumbria.
“A gem of a gallery. Can recommend a visit — Lucian Freud, Stanley Spencer, Celia Paul, Mary Fedden and Winifred Nicholson all on show!”  — Abbot Hall review

How to get here
By car
From the south
Exit the M6 at junction 36 and follow the A590 to South Lakes. Take the South Kendal (A6) exit and follow the signs for the town centre. Abbot Hall Art Gallery is the first right after the Parish Church. Kendal has a one-way system so if you miss the turn, you need to loop around the town via Aynam Road.
From the north
Exit the M6 at junction 37, turning onto the A684. Follow signs to Kendal. Turn left onto Park Side Road. At the end of the road turn right onto Lound Road. Stay in the right-hand lane as you cross the small bridge over the River Kent.   Keep in the right-hand lane as you enter the town centre. Abbot Hall Art Gallery is the first right after the Parish Church. Kendal has a one ways system so if you miss the turn, you need to loop around the town via Aynam Road.
Parking
There is a small onsite pay and display car park at Abbot Hall with Blue Badge spaces. This is operated by Cumbria Parking Services.

By train
The nearest mainline railway station is Oxenholme. A connecting train will bring you to Kendal railway station. From here, Abbot Hall is one mile. Plan your journey by train .
By bus
Bus services run through Kendal connecting the town with the Lake District, Barrow and Lancaster.  Kirkland is the nearest bus stop. All traffic through the town centre is via a one-way system, so buses from Kendal Bus Station pass Abbot Hall on the other side of the river. Get off at the Weavers Court stop and use the footbridge opposite to get to the other side of the river. It takes about 6 minutes to walk via the riverside path to the gallery. Plan your journey by bus.
On foot
You can walk to Abbot Hall from the town centre in approximately 10 minutes either by the main street or along the riverside path. The path is a shared pedestrian and bicycle route.
