Unique, handmade Grasmere Gingerbread® is an iconic, delicious cross between a biscuit and a cake. Freshly baked and sold each day from a traditional, secret clean recipe at The Grasmere Gingerbread Shop – the top place to visit in the heart of the English Lake District – it may also be delivered straight to your door.
Our Lakeland spicy-sweet food sensation is enjoyed by King Charles and other world-famous celebrities. The ‘best gingerbread in the world’, the best cake in the world, the best biscuit in the world and the best food gift in Lakeland, this home-made heritage product is synonymous with efficient, personal and friendly service.
The star food gift in fantastic Grasmere Gingerbread® gift hampers, our truly delicious and affordable artisan goodie invented in 1854 by Victorian cook Sarah Nelson has stood the test of time. Step inside the historic, atmospheric Grasmere Gingerbread Shop or visit our online shop to buy artisan Grasmere Gingerbread® and many other exclusive ginger-themed foodstuffs including conserves, chocolates, toffee, drinks and rum butter.
