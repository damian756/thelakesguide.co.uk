Home > Visit > Opening Hours & Admission
Keswick Museum is set across three galleries, including a main room of geology, archaeology and Lakeland curiosities; our special exhibitions room, currently housing Outside In , an immersive landscape experience; and the Community Gallery , a dedicated exhibition space for local groups, organisations, and individuals to showcase their work, stories, and heritage.
All tickets are valid for 12 months, allowing you to come back and enjoy our changing exhibitions throughout the year.

Carers
Free entry to the museum is available for carers when accompanying the person they support.
Proof of entitlement may include:
Current letter or card that shows you receive carers allowance/credit
Current letter or card that shows the person you look after receives allowance/credit eg PIP, disability living allowance, attendance allowance etc
Carer ID cards (eg issued by national schemes or councils) that specify essential companion

Your Support Keeps Keswick Museum Here
All proceeds from admission tickets are used to support the activities of Keswick Museum, an independent charitable organisation.
Protecting our historic building
Preserving our collection
Funding our social outreach programmes
Supporting opportunities for volunteering in our community
By buying a ticket, visiting our shop or making a donation, you are supporting all that Keswick Museum does to share the heritage of the Lakes and safeguard it for future generations.
Thank you for your support!
