Ullswater Buses - Explore the area during your visit
Ullswater is the second largest lake in the Lake District. In addition to our very popular service 508 and its open top buses, we have introduced even more services to make travelling to, from and along this fantastic valley a sustainable and stress-free adventure. This majestic 7.3-mile ribbon of water flanked by the most impressive fells is in our opinion the hidden gem of the Lake District. Its pureness and tranquillity a breath of fresh air for those seeking to avoid the hustle and bustle of some of its more well known counterparts.
Use this page for inspiration as to how to plan your trip by bus.

Aira Force Waterfall
Travel direct by bus from Penrith, Keswick, Howtown & Ullswater Accommodation
A 150m waterfall 1km from the shores of the Lake.

Helvellyn
Travel direct by bus from Penrith & Windermere
If you have a head for heights, the infamous route up Helvellyn from Glenridding is easily accessible by bus. Not for the faint hearted, the round of Striding Edge and Swirral Edge is a superb route, a must do for experienced walkers with the right gear. Also consider a descent into Thirlmere to join service 555.
508
Ullswater Steamers
Travel direct by bus from Keswick, Penrith & Windermere
Enjoy a unique experience on board one of the largest heritage vessel fleets in the world.
508
Pooley Bridge
Travel direct by bus from Keswick, Penrith & Windermere
The central hub of Ullswater, home to an array of pubs, tea rooms, restaurants & hotels. An ideal starting point to explore the valley
508
Dalemain Historic Mansion & Gardens
Walk from Pooley Bridge, travel direct by bus from Keswick, Penrith & Windermere
Home of the World Marmalade Awards & Festival, this historic mansion and gardens is worth a visit, in particular to see it's collection of fine art and furniture.

Ullswater Steamers operate from four piers along the lake, Pooley Bridge, Howtown, Aira Force and Glenridding.
An alternative perspective of the lake can be seen from the water. Why not combine with a bus trip with our range of combined tickets.
The Bus & Boat tickets includes any o ne way single cruise  journey from any of the four piers at Pooley Bridge, Howtown, Glenridding or Aira Force plus unlimited all day bus travel within the Explorer Zone.

Bus & Boot
It’s possible to travel around Ullswater sustainably via a myriad of well-marked footpaths allowing you to combine walk and bus for some fantastic circular itineraries. Our favourites are;
Ullswater Way – A 20 mile walking route around the lake. An easy to walk route ideal for all ages is easily split up into smaller sections when combined with bus or boat.
Askham Fell, A fantastic walk over Askham fell allows an alternative option of getting between Pooley Bridge and Askham(close to Lowther Castle) with stunning vistas over Ullswater. Easy going paths suitable for all levels of experience.
Eamont Way – Opened in 2023 the Eamont Way is the active travel alternative when travelling between Penrith and Pooley Bridge. 5.5miles through open fields, woodlands and meadows following the course of the River Eamont.
Bus & Bike
It’s possible to travel around Ullswater sustainably via bike hire;
Ullswater eBikes are offering a 5% discount per eBike for anyone who arrives via the 508 service. Show your bus ticket with the same date as the hire date to redeem the discount!
Bike hire is also available at Lowther & Arragons in Penrith
