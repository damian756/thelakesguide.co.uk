Fellpack Restaurant
Our signature restaurant near Keswick is made for celebration. Discover an unparalleled dining experience paired with remarkable views over the surrounding fells and sweeping valleys.
Ten years ago, our story began in a modest bistro in the heart of Keswick’s cobbled streets. A decade later, following hundreds of menu iterations and two relocations, our Fellpack restaurant is the best reflection of how we think dining should be.
The seasonal menu delicately balances comfort with experiential flavours, with every item on the menu skilfully crafted by our chefs.
Before or after your meal, wander into the bar, relax in the vinyl lounge or unwind in the games room.
Lose track of time and let us spoil you.
