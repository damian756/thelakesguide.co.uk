Aira Force Car Park
Type: Car Park
Watermillock , Cumbria , CA11 0JS
About
Pay & Display car park with 130 spaces.
Public toilets located adjacent to the car park.
This car park is operated by The National Trust. For all enquiries, please use the details below:
Tel: 01768 482067
Visit Website
Located on the junction of the A592 and the A5091. During busy times (bank holidays, school holidays and sunny weekends) the car park usually fills up around 10am, so arriving early is recommended.
The main waterfall is a 1 mile round trip from this car park but longer walks are available. Tea-room on site, serving take away drinks, snacks and ice cream, please check opening times before you visit. Toilets open daily 10am – 4pm (closed periodically to allow for enhanced cleaning). Payment for parking is accepted by coins, via the Pay by Phone App or by card at the Welcome Building or Tea-room during opening hours. Sat Nav: CA11 0JS.
