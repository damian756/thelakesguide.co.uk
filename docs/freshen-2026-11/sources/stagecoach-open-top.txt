Enjoy the Lakes by our Open Top Buses
Enjoy the Lakes from our open top buses
Our very popular open top buses allow you to leave the car behind and let us be your driver for the day. With the £3 capped fare , see the Lake District in a different way. A fun, refreshing way to see the Lakes, our 599 open top buses cover the Central Lakes (Bowness, Windermere, Ambleside, Grasmere)
Travelling around the Lake District?
Download the Lakes by Bus Guide 📖❄️
Travel from Bowness to Grasmere   on the  599  ☀️
The open top  599  runs every hour from Bowness to Grasmere , connecting with mainline station, Windermere.
We provide USB charging points, spaces for buggies and bikes and audio commentary, so you get even more from your trip.
