Our Tea Rooms
Our Traditional Tea Rooms are situated on the Keswick and Bowness sites above our shops where you will always receive a warm welcome.
Because of its popularity and our preparation of fresh food there can be a short wait at busy times to get customers seated. If this happens we ask for your indulgence.
Bowness on Windermere Tea Room
Experience our traditional tea room above our Bowness-on-Windermere shop. Serving a range of snacks to meals, along with our Cream Teas.

Keswick Shop, Tea Room and Coffee Bar
Our world-famous Keswick tea room serves a full range of food and drinks, from light snacks through to full meals.
