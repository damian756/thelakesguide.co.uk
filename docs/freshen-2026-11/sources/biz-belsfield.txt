Escape to the iconic Belsfield Hotel Windermere at the heart of the
English Lake District
A warm welcome awaits at our 4-star heritage hotel with unmatched views of Windermere and the Lakeland fells beyond.  Our historic interiors combine with elegant bedrooms and modern menus to offer a unique and unforgettable stay in Bowness-on-Windermere just steps from the shore of England’s largest lake.
Book a room
Elegant bedrooms
Whether you have travelled from near or afar, from the moment you step into our hotel, our dedicated, friendly and helpful team is on-hand to ensure your every need is met. With 62 bedrooms and suites each promising timeless elegance and a sense of relaxed calm, choose a Lake View room and take in the glorious vista from your private space or opt for an outlook over our extensive gardens.
Book a room
Dining with a view
Whether you choose our classic Afternoon Tea, a relaxed Lunch or Dinner, our Brasserie offers semi-formal dining with lake views. Before dining, enjoy drinks in our Art Deco Bar then retire later to our Drawing Room. Our outdoor Terrace is always open and offers unrivalled views of Windermere as the sun sets behind the fells. For breakfast, indulge in our hot and continental buffet in the elegant, period surroundings of our Ballroom restaurant.
