Delightful Pub with Rooms
Nestled in the heart of Ambleside, The Unicorn Inn is a quintessential traditional Lakeland pub, perfectly positioned for exploring the breathtaking beauty of the Lake District. Surrounded by stunning fells and scenic landscapes, it offers a warm, welcoming base whether you are visiting for adventure, relaxation, or a short break away.
Blending classic charm with modern comfort, The Unicorn Inn provides comfortable accommodation right in the centre of town, making it ideal for walkers, families, couples, and business travellers alike. After a day exploring the lakes and mountains, unwind in a cosy, characterful setting where you can enjoy good food, a friendly atmosphere, and a true taste of Lakeland hospitality.
Enjoy the comfort of home-cooked food at The Unicorn Inn, Ambleside, where traditional pub favourites are prepared with care and served in a warm, welcoming setting. Our menu offers a variety of tasty, satisfying dishes throughout the week, perfect after a day exploring the Lake District fells.
Whether you are stopping by for a relaxed lunch or a hearty evening meal, you can pair your food with a perfectly poured pint of Robinsons beer , adding to the authentic pub experience. With its friendly atmosphere and classic Lakeland charm, The Unicorn Inn is the ideal place to unwind, refuel, and enjoy great food in the heart of Ambleside.
