About The Dog & Gun, Keswick
The Dog & Gun is a great local pub in the heart of the community, with friendly service and honest pricing. The Dog and Gun is famous for its homemade hungarian Goulash which has been made here for years and comes in small and large portions. We are proud of the fact that we do not sell any shallow or deep fried food. Show full description
