A relaxed place for fun and adventure
Make yourself at home in our flexible eating and play areas. Carers can relax with a coffee while under 8s enjoy safe adventures at our new Indoor Play area. We encourage noise and play in our toy shop, too, with its large collection of Lego alongside puzzles and pocket money treats. Our Cafe has a children’s menu, perfect for enjoying before or after a family movie on our giant-screen Cinema.

A stimulating mini-world of slides, tunnels, tents and a ball pool, with dedicated zone for under 5s.  For the safety of visitors and colleagues, Indoor Play slots are booked in advance to limit numbers. The Play area is thoroughly cleaned between each one hour session. Max height 137cm.

Birthday Parties!
Our new Children’s Film Party Package includes tickets to a film screening of your choice from our latest Cinema schedule with a hotdog and chips for only £13.50 per child (minimum of 10 required), which you can enjoy in our designated children’s area. You can also bring along a birthday cake too! Make the day even more special with our add-ons, including Karma Sodas for £2.95.
Want a specific classic like Inside Out 2 or Zootropolis? We can arrange it! Additional costs start from £150 plus a £60 handling fee. Private screenings are also available upon request, starting at £250 + VAT (plus any film hire costs as applicable).
To book a birthday to remember, just email enquiries@rheged.com to lock in your date!
