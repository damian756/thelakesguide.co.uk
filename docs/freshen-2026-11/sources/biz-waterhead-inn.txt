Set on the shores of Lake Windermere with Waterhead Pier directly opposite, The Waterhead Inn is a true lakeside base in the heart of the Lake District. Just a short stroll from Ambleside village centre, our Ambleside hotel on Lake Windermere brings together the character of a traditional pub with rooms and an unbeatable waterfront setting.
Ideal for couples seeking a scenic Lake District break, families planning a UK staycation, and dog owners looking for a pet-friendly escape, The Waterhead Inn offers comfort, convenience and sought-after lake views. Step outside to explore boat trips, watersports and fell walks around Lake Windermere , or settle in for a relaxed pub stay with everything in one place.
With excellent public transport links from the door and easy access to walking trails and local attractions, it’s a well-located hotel in Ambleside, Cumbria for exploring more of the national park. Enjoy hearty pub food and well-kept drinks served all day, every day, with a welcoming team ready to help you switch off and settle in.

Rooms at The Waterhead Inn
Check in from 3pm
Wondering why you should book a stay here at The Waterhead Inn? Perhaps it’s because of our iconic lakeside setting by Waterhead Pier, at the northern tip of Lake Windermere , offering uninterrupted views, a welcoming pub atmosphere and comfortable rooms right by the picturesque water.
Overlooking England’s largest lake and just moments from Ambleside, our inn is perfectly placed for scenic Lake District breaks. Step outside for boat trips and fell walks, then return to relaxed dining, well-kept drinks and a cosy place to stay – all under one roof. It’s everything you need from a pub with rooms in the Lake District.
We’re proud to offer dog-friendly accommodation, so four-legged companions are welcome to join your lakeside escape. Simply add this option during the booking process, where up to two dogs can stay with you for just £15 per night.
On-site parking is available for both our main inn rooms and Lakehouse rooms opposite (please note there is an incline to access the Lakehouse, which may not be suitable for guests with limited mobility).
Whether you’re planning a short staycation, a walking break or a laid-back weekend by the water, booking direct guarantees the best price and access to exclusive offers.
Please note, images showcase room types as accurately as possible, however rooms do slightly vary. Contact our central reservations team on 0191 5803610 for more information. Rooms at the Lakehouse are situated in an annex building, located across the road from The Waterhead Inn. Check in, check out, breakfast and all other meals are located at The Waterhead Inn.

Join us for brunch, lunch or dinner and enjoy all-day food and drink in one of the best lake view pub settings in the Lake District.
Whether it’s a relaxed coffee by the water, a family meal after a day outdoors or an evening drink as the sun sets over the lake, The Waterhead Inn brings together great pub food, fantastic drinks and an unbeatable Windermere backdrop.

The Waterhead Inn FAQs
Heading to The Waterhead Inn for your next break? Our FAQs will help you prepare with essential information on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and our full range of guest amenities.
Is parking available at The Waterhead Inn?
Yes, free on-site parking is available for guests at The Waterhead Inn and at the Lakehouse rooms opposite. This makes it easy to explore Ambleside, Lake Windermere , and the surrounding Lake District without worrying about your car. Please note that access to the Lakehouse rooms has an incline, which may not be suitable for guests with mobility constraints.

What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at The Waterhead Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is disabled access into The Waterhead Inn from the restaurant doors. There is one accessible room however there are a few steps to and from the room. Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm. For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Is The Waterhead Inn dog-friendly?
Yes, your dogs are more than welcome at The Waterhead Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms in a select number of rooms across our inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability, and so we can allocate you accordingly. When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610, or ensure you add this option on during the online booking process.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Waterhead Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.   We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Do you take table bookings at the pub?
Yes, The Waterhead Inn accepts table bookings online for lunch, dinner, and brunch – simply click here to reserve your spot. Walk-ins are always welcome too, so feel free to drop by and enjoy hearty pub food and drinks with stunning views of Lake Windermere .
Table Booking Terms & Conditions:
Advance bookings: Tables can be reserved online from until 3 hours before the selected booking time.
Walk-ins: Walk-ins are always welcome and subject to availability.
Deposits: In some cases, a deposit may be required (if table is over 8 people) . This is managed directly by the individual inn, and a member of the team will be in touch if a deposit is needed.
Arrival time: Tables will be held for 15 minutes after the booked time. After this, the table may be released.
Larger groups: Bookings for 9 or more guests will be submitted as an enquiry. The relevant inn will review your enquiry and accept or reject based on availability.
Cancellations: Please let us know if you need to cancel your booking. Advance notice helps us plan and offer the table to other guests.

Is The Waterhead Inn family friendly?

What is the Lakehouse and where is it located?
The Lakehouse is our charming annex building, just across the road from the main inn, offering extra rooms for your stay. Parking is available on-site for both Lakehouse and main inn guests. Please be aware there’s an incline to reach the Lakehouse, so it may not be ideal for guests with limited mobility. All check-in, check-out, breakfast, and pub facilities are conveniently located at The Waterhead Inn, so you can enjoy everything we offer just a short walk away.

Does The Waterhead Inn offer food and drink all day?
Yes, The Waterhead Inn serves food and drink all day, making it a perfect spot for visitors to Ambleside and Lake Windermere. Start your morning with a full Cumbrian breakfast, served from 7:30am to 11:00am, enjoy freshly prepared brunch and lunch options, or settle in for hearty pub fayre in the evening with our seasonal menu available from 12:00pm to 9:00pm. Drinks are served daily from 11:00am to 11:00pm.
Our menu features classic British and Cumbrian dishes, seasonal specials, and indulgent desserts, all served in a relaxed lakeside pub setting. Whether you’re staying overnight or visiting just for a meal, The Waterhead Inn offers all-day dining with stunning lake views and a fantastic selection of drinks available throughout the day.

What types of rooms and accommodation are available at The Waterhead Inn?
The Waterhead Inn offers a range of comfortable accommodation in Ambleside, designed to suit couples, families, solo trips and groups visiting the Lake District. Guests can choose from classic double and twin rooms, or treat themselves to a superior room with stunning lake views, perfect for a romantic getaway or a relaxing stay by Lake Windermere . The Lakehouse, handily located across the road, offers additional rooms in an annex building. When staying in a Lakehouse room, check in, check out, breakfast and any other meals are all located at The Waterhead Inn.
Whether you’re looking for a traditional pub room or lakeside accommodation with exceptional views, The Waterhead Inn offers options that combine comfort, style, and a prime location for exploring the Lake District.
