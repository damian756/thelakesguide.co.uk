Unlimited boat travel for 48 hours
Our best value ticket is only available during the winter season and gives you 48 hour's worth of travel on our red cruise , yellow cruise and the islands cruise .
It's ideal for anyone wanting to explore Windermere at their own pace or who's visiting the Lake District on a weekend or short break. This ticket is valid for 48 hours from the first time that you use it and can be used on any 2 days of the week.
More information:
Your ticket is valid on all of the following cruises:
Full length of Windermere | Lakeside ↔ Ambleside (Waterhead) – combining Red & Yellow Cruises
Red Cruise | Half of the lake: Bowness → Windermere Jetty Museum → Brockhole → Ambleside (Waterhead) → Bowness
Yellow Cruise | Half of the lake: Bowness ↔ Lakeside
Islands (Blue) Cruise | Circular trip from Bowness

Departure Points
Start this cruise from any of these locations
Bowness
As our busiest pier, Bowness offers the widest variety of cruises on the lake.
Located at postcode LA23 3HQ , it's a 2-minute walk from Bowness village and the closest pier to Windermere Village (10 minutes by bus/car or a 25-minute walk).
While we don't have our own parking, there are over 400 pay-and-display spaces within walking distance. You'll also find easy access to bus connections and national rail services via Windermere Railway Station.

Ambleside
Located at the northern tip of Windermere, Ambleside (Waterhead) Pier offers some of the most stunning views of the Lake District fells.
The pier's postcode is LA22 0EY . It's an easy, flat walk just a half-mile from Ambleside village center, with regular buses also available.
While the pier has a small car park, there are over 200 public pay-and-display spaces within a five-minute walk. You'll also find easy access to local bus connections.

Lakeside
Located at the southernmost tip of Windermere, Lakeside is our quietest pier, and home to some of the area's family-friendly attractions.
The pier's postcode is LA12 8AS and has over 300 on-site pay and display car parking spaces at a reasonable price.
It provides direct access to the Lakeside & Haverthwaite Railway (seasonal) , but bus services are limited with the nearest bus stop being at Newby Bridge (1.5 miles away via footpath).

Brockhole
Brockhole is more than just a pier; it's the perfect destination for family fun - lakeshore walks, adventure play areas and Zip World .
The pier's closest postcode is LA23 1LJ but is only accessible by gravel footpath at the bottom of Brockhole's gardens.
The on-site car park can get busy, but it has great bus connections with a bus top outside on the busy A591 - about a 10-minute walk from our pier on the lakeshore.

Windermere Jetty (Museum)
Our pier at Windermere Jetty Museum is attached to the award-winning museum  dedicated to the boats and rich history of the lake.
The pier is located at postcode LA23 1BN , on the eastern shore of Windermere. As well as boat access, the museum can be reached on foot from Bowness Pier and it takes about 20-minutes.
The museum has limited on-site pay-and-display parking, but bus routes from the village are 10-minutes walk away and Windermere Railway Station is about a 25-minute walk away.
