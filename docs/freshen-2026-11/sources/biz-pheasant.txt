A charming 17th-century coaching inn, The Pheasant Inn combines outstanding hospitality, local character, and a truly breathtaking Lake District setting. Nestled beneath Sale Fell, and just moments from Bassenthwaite Lake, the inn is perfectly positioned for exploring the natural beauty of The Lake District National Park.Whether you’re setting out on lakeside walks, hill climbs, or scenic drives, The Pheasant Inn offers an ideal base to return to at the end of the day. Inside, guests will find a warm, welcoming atmosphere with plenty of cosy corners to relax in.
Renovated in 2021, the inn has carefully retained the charm built up over than 200 years, blending historic character with modern character. Known for its dog-friendly welcome, The Pheasant Inn is a favourite with walkers and their four-legged companions, while our locally inspired menus celebrate the very best food and drink Cumbria has to offer.

Rooms at The Pheasant Inn
Check in from 3pm
Offering 26 individually styled bedrooms, The Pheasant Inn provides a comfortable and characterful place to stay in the heart of the northern Lake District. Each room is thoughtfully equipped with essential amenities, and guests benefit from free on-site parking, making it an ideal base for exploring the surrounding area. With a selection of spacious rooms, the Pheasant is a comfortable choice for couples, families, and groups alike.
The Pheasant Inn is exceptionally dog friendly, welcoming four-legged companions as warmly as their owners. Our dog-friendly rooms allow you to enjoy the Lake District with your canine companion in tow. With scenic walks right on the doorstep and plenty of space to unwind after a day outdoors, it’s an ideal choice for dog-friendly stays in Cumbria. Simply add this option during the booking process, where up to two dogs can stay with you for just £15 per night.
Please note, images showcase room types as accurately as possible, however rooms do slightly vary. Contact our central reservations team on 0191 5803610 for more information.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Bassenthwaite
The Pheasant Inn makes it easy to enjoy a Lake District getaway with your dog. As a dog friendly hotel in Bassenthwaite, we have dog-friendly rooms where your pooch can settle in after a day of exploring. Just add them to your booking when you reserve your room.
Up to two dogs can stay for £15 per night when booking direct. They’re also welcome in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way!
With the Lake District right on your doorstep, there are plenty of scenic spots and walking routes to explore together before heading back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Bassenthwaite or a dog friendly accommodation near Bassenthwaite Lake, the Pheasant Inn is a great base for a full-family getaway!

Food & Drinks in Basenthwaite
Food service: 7:30am – 9:00pm daily
For comforting food and drink in Bassenthwaite, there’s always a warm welcome waiting at The Pheasant Inn. Whether you’re calling in after a lakeside walk or settling in for a relaxed evening meal, our restaurant is a place to slow down, unwind, and enjoy goof food.
Our menus are inspired by the best of Cumbria, with locally sourced ingredients and seasonal produce. By working closely with trusted Cumbrian suppliers, our chefs create wholesome, flavourful dishes that celebrate local flavours and classic comforts.
Sundays at The Pheasant Inn are a firm favourite with guests and locals alike. Our traditional Sunday roasts are made for lingering afternoons, with your choice of beef, pork or a vegetarian option, served with crip roast potatoes, seasonal vegetables, and plenty of rich gravy.
The restaurant itself is full of character, offering a relaxed, warm atmosphere where muddy boots and wagging tails are always welcome. Pair your meal with a carefully chosen, selection of local ales, wines, and spirits, and you have the perfect ending to a day in the Lake District.

Do you have on site parking available?
There is free on site car paking available at The Pheasant Inn. Parking is first come first served.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Pheasant Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We offer a select number of pet-friendly hotel rooms, so your dogs can join you on overnight stays. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability, and so we can allocate you accordingly. When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.
Close
What facilities are provided in the rooms?
All rooms at The Pheasant Inn provide complimentary Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV, and tea & coffee making facilities. Please note, we do not provide travel cots. If you plan on bringing your own, we recommend speaking with our reservations team on 0191 580 3610 to ensure the room you’re booking will comfortably accommodate one.
Close
What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Pheasant Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.   We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Do you take table bookings?
Table bookings can be made online at The Pheasant Inn by clicking here .  Walk-ins are always welcome so please pop in, we’d love to see you!
Table Booking Terms & Conditions:
Advance bookings: Tables can be reserved online from until 3 hours before the selected booking time.
Walk-ins: Walk-ins are always welcome and subject to availability.
Deposits: In some cases, a deposit may be required (if table is over 8 people) . This is managed directly by the individual inn, and a member of the team will be in touch if a deposit is needed.
Arrival time: Tables will be held for 15 minutes after the booked time. After this, the table may be released.
Larger groups: Bookings for 9 or more guests will be submitted as an enquiry. The relevant inn will review your enquiry and accept or reject based on availability.
Cancellations: Please let us know if you need to cancel your booking. Advance notice helps us plan and offer the table to other guests.
