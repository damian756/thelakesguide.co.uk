The Brewery
The company owners all share a passion for real ale
and were keen to re-establish a local brewery. Kirkby Lonsdale has had brewing at its heart for
centuries until the trade had all but died out.

Kirkby Lonsdale Coffee Roasters
While our delicious beans are ethically plucked from some of the planet’s most exotic destinations, our inspiration is firmly rooted in the soil of our home town, roasting in the Royal Barn a local building steeped in history.

How to find us
Leave the M6 at Junction 36 following signs for Kirkby Lonsdale A65, follow this road until you reach the roundabout parallel to Booths supermarket. Take the first exit toward booths and turn right at the end of the road. We are situated on the right hand side of the road opposite the Post Office next to New Road Car park. Our address is: New Rd, Kirkby Lonsdale, LA6 2AB.

The Orange Tree
Choose from a selection of our luxury rooms which are all en-suite, contain a flat-screen LCD TV, and provide hot beverage facilities. Visit the Orange Tree website here.
Outhouse Bothy
Hidden in the centre of one of the most desirable places to live in England. The Outhouse Bothy is a teeny tiny one-bedroom dwelling. The first thing you ll notice is the ancient (uneven) curved stone staircase, unsuitable for children. The ground floor has a dinky kitchen, microwave, small fridge, kettle, toaster & of course a kitchen sink (great excuse not to cook) The first-floor bedroom opens out to a sun trapped terrace with adjoining WC & shower room.
