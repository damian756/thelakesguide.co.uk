Let's Go Car Free in the Lake District, Cumbria
Welcome to the Lake District, Cumbria: a place which has it all, from stunning natural landscapes to  world-class food and drink . Here, lakes, mountains, forests and coast all combine to create a destination you’ll want to return to time and time again. From a famously warm welcome to fantastic attractions; the great outdoors to arts, culture and heritage, there are so many reasons to visit. Plus, did we mention it’s easy to get here and travel around without your car?
Getting to the Lake District, Cumbria by train is quick and easy from a number of cities including London, Manchester, Liverpool, Glasgow and Edinburgh, with many direct services transporting you to this beautiful part of the country:
Glasgow to Carlisle in approx 1 hour 20 minutes
London Euston to Oxenholme, Lake District in 2  hours 45 minutes.
Manchester to Lake District in 1 hour 30 minutes
Once you’re here, it’s simple and straightforward to continue your journey on public transport, as Stagecoach run regular bus services to our beautiful towns and villages. Sit back, relax, and take in the stunning scenery along the way. Some routes feature open top buses, and the views are simply breathtaking.

Cumbria Travel Pass: The Ultimate All-in-One Ticket
Exploring the region car-free has never been simpler. The Cumbria Travel Pass gives you unlimited, seamless travel across Northern, TransPennine Express, and Avanti trains, Stagecoach buses, and Windermere Lake Cruises.
The pass also includes discounts on heritage railways and further lake cruises (including Ravenglass & Eskdale Railway, Coniston Launch and Ullswater Steamers).
Price: £40 for a 1 Day Pass | £99 for a 3 Day Pass
Good to know: Children travel for half price (under 5s free) and railcard discounts still apply
Please note: This pass replaces the previous individual Day Ranger ticket

Car-free Adventures
Arrive by train and explore by bus. These sustainable travel itineraries are specially designed to help you discover the Lake District, Cumbria car-free.
Avanti West Coast
Avanti West Coast runs fast and frequent trains from London, Birmingham and Glasgow to the Lake District, serving Oxenholme (South Lakes), Penrith (North Lakes) & Carlisle.
Northern Trains
Northern Trains plays a vital role connecting tens of thousands of people every day. From the high-speed London to Scotland West Coast Main line, you can hop off at Oxenholme onto a Northern service that takes you to the heart of the lakes at Windermere.
Stagecoach
Go car-free and explore the Lake District, Cumbria by bus.
TransPennine Express
TransPennine Express runs regular services to Carlisle, Penrith and Oxenholme for the Lake District, from Manchester Airport, Manchester, Liverpool, Preston, Glasgow and Edinburgh.
Find your perfect place to stay
If you are looking for the best accommodation in the Lake District, you will be spoilt for choice. Choose from exclusive hotels, the best B&Bs, cosy Lake District cottages, unique glamping experiences and well-equipped campsites.
