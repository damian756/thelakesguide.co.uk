OUR STORY
Welcome to our quirky little cafe bar! Swing by and grab yourself a table. We don’t take any reservations, just pop in and enjoy a handmade pizza & a nice cold beer or drink!
The “Squorange”, as it’s affectionately known by locals and visitors alike, is an unexpected gem found nestled in the heart of Keswick. With a full bar, specialty coffee menu freshly made stone-baked pizzas, and live music evenings; the warm and welcoming atmosphere is one you will find hard to leave!
With guest wines from family run vineyards, local microbreweries offered alongside Belgian and German beers, rotating coffee blends from Cumbrian roasteries as well as sourcing further afield flavours from India & South America, we pride ourselves on using a wide range of authentic and high quality suppliers.
So why not join us for a catch up with a pot of tea and a slice of cake or enjoy a perfect evening with a much loved cocktail and freshly made pizza from our hot stone pizza oven or on a rainy day sit back and enjoy an eclectic mix of flavours from our European inspired tapas menu over a game of chess. There are a whole host of experiences to be had and whether you are looking for some quiet time or an opportunity to meet or even make new friends, we look forward to welcoming you soon.
