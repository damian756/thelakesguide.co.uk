Plan your visit to our heritage tourist attraction
Ullswater ‘Steamers’ is a dog-friendly Lake District visitor attraction that offers the perfect day out for the whole family. We operate a variety of lake cruises and boat trips linking iconic walking routes, waterfalls, and Lakeland villages from 20 minutes to two hours, depending on your choice of journey. Open 363 days a year except Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. It’s the perfect option for those who are looking for things to do in the Lake District!
Good things to know about Ullswater ‘Steamers’:
We recommend booking day tickets online in advance of your journey. Daily service tickets can also be purchased at the pier houses or onboard the boats.
The four piers link to spectacular walking routes connecting you to each destination point around the Ullswater Way
Explore the magic and mystery of the National Trust Aira Force waterfalls. Cruise connections from April – October.
Our added-value season tickets offer unlimited travel on all scheduled sailings across Ullswater for a full year from the date of purchase.
Scan the QR codes around the Ullswater Way and explore the virtual art gallery here
Please call ahead if you require wheelchair access. Due to the heritage nature of our vessels and the waterside location, we regret that our attraction is not suitable for motorised wheelchairs or mobility scooters. We have wheelchairs available for use that can be reserved.
Electric car charging points at Glenridding pier. We recommend downloading the app before your visit.
Our main pier houses are located at Glenridding and Pooley Bridge. From Pooley Bridge village car parks, it is a 10 minute walk to the pier. Drop off point outside the door.
We have a range of cruise options around the lake, from 20 minutes to over two hours.
